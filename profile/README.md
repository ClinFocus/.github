# ClinFocus

**Clinical data. Systems. Intelligence.**

ClinFocus is a data-first clinical research organization helping sponsors build, manage, integrate, and understand the data that drives clinical development.

Our work spans clinical data management, biometrics, statistical programming, eClinical systems, analytics, and responsible AI — with a focus on reliable data, compliant systems, and technology that supports better decisions throughout the clinical trial lifecycle.

## What we do

**Clinical Data & Biometrics**
Data management and statistical programming focused on data integrity, reliable outputs, and submission-ready deliverables.

**EDC & Clinical Systems**
Design, build, and implementation of scalable clinical studies and supporting clinical technology.

**Data Integration & Analytics**
Integration of EDC, CTMS, laboratory, safety, and other clinical data sources to enable centralized analytics and operational insight.

**AI Enablement**
Governance, validated tools, automation, and applied AI designed for responsible use within clinical research workflows.

**Clinical Technology**
Development of software and automation that improve how clinical teams collect, process, review, and work with study data.

## Engineering at ClinFocus

This organization supports ClinFocus software development and technical initiatives across clinical data, automation, and clinical systems.

Our engineering work focuses on building reliable, maintainable solutions that improve clinical research workflows and support the teams working with clinical data.
